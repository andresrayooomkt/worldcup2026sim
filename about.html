What is This?

World Cup 2026 Simulator is a free, interactive tool that lets you predict every single match of the 2026 FIFA World Cup — all 104 games across the group stage, Round of 32, Round of 16, Quarterfinals, Semifinals, and the Grand Final at MetLife Stadium in New York/New Jersey.

You can enter your own scores manually for each match or let the simulator generate random results based on team strength ratings. Watch the group standings update in real time, see the knockout bracket fill automatically, and celebrate your predicted champion with a confetti animation.

Key Features

  • All 104 matches with real dates, venues, and kick-off times from the official FIFA schedule
  • 48 teams including the final qualifiers (Bosnia, Türkiye, Sweden, Czechia, DR Congo, Iraq)
  • Manual or simulated scores — your predictions, your way
  • Live group standings that update as you fill in results
  • Auto-populated knockout brackets based on group results, including best third-placed teams
  • Champion celebration with animation and confetti when you decide the final
  • 16 real venues across Mexico, USA, and Canada

Who Made This?

This simulator was built by an independent developer and football fan from Mexico. It was created as a passion project to help fans around the world engage with the tournament, make predictions, and share their brackets with friends.

The project is not affiliated with any commercial entity, betting company, or official organization. It is purely for entertainment and educational purposes.

How Accurate is the Simulator?

The random simulation engine uses a weighted system based on approximate team strength ratings. These are not official FIFA rankings — they are estimates designed to produce realistic-looking results. The real fun is in entering your own predictions manually and seeing how your bracket plays out.
