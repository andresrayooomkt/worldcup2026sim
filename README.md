---

## 🏆 What is This?

An interactive, browser-based simulator for the **2026 FIFA World Cup** hosted across **Mexico 🇲🇽, USA 🇺🇸 and Canada 🇨🇦**.

Simulate the entire tournament match by match — manually pick your scores or let the engine generate results based on team strength ratings. Watch group standings update in real time, see the knockout bracket fill automatically, and find out who lifts the trophy at MetLife Stadium on July 19.

Built as a passion project by a football fan and developer from Mexico. No backend. No login. Just football.

## 🎮 How to Use

1. **Groups tab** — Fill in match results manually or hit **Simulate All** for instant results
2. **Group tabs (A–L)** — Drill into each group to see standings and individual match cards
3. **Knockout stage** — Bracket auto-populates based on your group results
4. **Simulate All → Current Phase** — Jump straight to the next unplayed round
5. **Share** — Export your prediction from any match card
